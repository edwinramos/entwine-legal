Delete your data

Last updated: October 21, 2026

From the app (immediate)

  1. Open Entwine and go to Settings.
  2. Tap “Leave couple and delete data” and confirm.
This instantly deletes: the shared room (nicknames, challenge and progress, custom challenges, Vault memories, notification tokens), the pairing code, your anonymous identifier and all data stored on the device. The room is deleted for both members of the couple.

Without access to the app

If you already uninstalled the app or lost your phone, email edwinramos.93@gmail.com with the subject “Entwine - Delete data” and include your room’s pairing code (e.g. V9K-42M) if you remember it. We will delete that room’s data within 30 days. Uninstalling the app alone does not delete the room data in the cloud.

What is kept

Duo plan purchase records are kept by Google Play and RevenueCat according to their legal obligations. To cancel the subscription go to Google Play > Payments & subscriptions; deleting your data does not cancel the subscription automatically.
