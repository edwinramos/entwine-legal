Privacy policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Who is responsible

Entwine is developed by Edwin Ramos Madera, an independent developer (the “developer”). Contact: edwinramos.93@gmail.com.

Summary

Entwine shows no ads, uses no analytics or tracking SDKs, does not sell data and does not create email/password accounts. The app works with an anonymous identifier and a shared “room” between exactly two devices.

Data we process

  • Nickname you type when you start (e.g. “Love”). Stored on your device and in the shared room so your partner can see it.
  • Couple content: the active challenge and its progress, custom challenges you create, Vault memories (text), the chosen tone and shared settings (discreet mode, pause).
  • Anonymous identifier from Firebase Authentication (a random uid, no name or email) and the room’s 6-character pairing code.
  • Expo push notification token of each device, used to notify your partner.
  • Subscription status and purchase history for the Duo plan, handled by Google Play Billing. When the paying member has an active plan, the room stores a subscription flag (active or not, product, plan and the date it is valid until) that your partner can see; no purchase tokens or payment data are stored. The developer never receives your card details.
  • Account identifier for Google Play: when you buy, your anonymous uid is sent to Google Play as the purchase’s obfuscated account identifier.
  • Technical data logged by providers while delivering the service (e.g. IP address and device type).

Data we do NOT process

We do not access your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera. Unlocking the Vault with fingerprint, face or PIN is handled by the operating system; the app never receives biometric data.

Why we use it

Only to make the app work: sync the room in real time between the two devices, send reminders and partner notifications, and activate the Duo plan for both members of the room.

Where it is stored and who can see it

Room data is stored in Firebase Realtime Database (Google). Security rules only allow the two anonymous identifiers that form the room to read and write it. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and Google encrypts it at rest on its servers, but it is not end-to-end encrypted: it is technically accessible to the infrastructure provider. A copy is also kept in your device’s local storage.

Providers (processors)

  • Google Firebase (anonymous Authentication and Realtime Database) — privacy.
  • Expo (650 Industries) — push notification service, delivered through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — privacy.
  • Google Play (Google Play Billing) — subscription payments, billing and status — privacy.
We do not share data with anyone else or use it for advertising.

Retention

Room data is kept while the couple uses it. It is deleted immediately when either member uses “Leave couple and delete data” (in Settings), which deletes the room for both. If you request it by email, we will delete it within 30 days. Google Play keeps purchase records as long as their legal and accounting obligations require.

Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data at any time from the app or by writing to edwinramos.93@gmail.com. See how to delete your data.

Adults only

Entwine is intended exclusively for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from minors; if you believe a minor is using the app, contact us and we will delete their data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page with its update date.
